# Code of conduct

Synod welcomes respectful participation from people with different levels of
experience and different technical perspectives.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good faith when possible.
- Respect privacy, attribution, and licensing boundaries.
- Make space for questions and disagreement without ridicule.
- Disclose conflicts of interest that materially affect a contribution.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through GitHub's private reporting or moderation tools.
Do not post sensitive personal information publicly. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, severity, repeated behavior,
and the safety of affected participants.
